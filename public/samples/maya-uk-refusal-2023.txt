UK Visas and Immigration. Refusal of Entry Clearance.
Date: 2 April 2023. Application: Standard Visitor (application made March 2023).

You have stated that you intend to visit the UK for two weeks for tourism. You have provided bank statements, however these show a large deposit on 1 March 2023 which is unexplained, and I am therefore not satisfied the funds are genuinely available to you. You have not provided evidence of your employment such as an employer letter confirming approved leave. I am therefore not satisfied that you have sufficient ties to your country of residence or that you will leave the UK at the end of your visit. Your application is refused under paragraph V 4.2 of Appendix Visitor.
